Albert Village Allotment Association Constitution

Name
The name of the Association is Albert Village Allotment Association (hereafter known as ‘the Association)

Allotments Location & Make Up
The location of Albert Village Allotments is noted in appendix 1 and consists of a designated area for the cultivation of vegetables, flowers, and fruits (hereafter known as the Allotments) and a second area designated as Freewoods (hereafter known as the Woodland).

The Allotments will be divided into plots and members may join the Association as a tenant on one or more of these plots as long as they abide by the ‘Constitution’, the ‘Rules of the Association’ and ‘Tenancy Agreement’
The Woodland will be maintained so as to form sufficient barrier between the Allotments and the local park area adjacent
The Woodland will be cultivated so as to become a worthwhile asset to the Association, other organisations and the local community

Objects
The Objects of the Association (hereafter known as ‘the Objects’) are:-

To promote the interests of all members in their gardening activities and to take joint action for the benefit and protection of members, the allotment plots and/or woodlands

To maintain harmonious and productive relationships with our landowners (The National Forest Company), our lease holders (Ashby Woulds Town Council) and the local community

To promote a sense of vibrant community; to be socially inclusive; to value sustainability; to have regard for environmental protection; to value biodiversity; to promote open communication

To promote the sharing of knowledge about gardening, woodlands and allotment management in order to promote success in our core task of growing vegetables, flowers and fruits and maintaining our woodlands

Aims
The Aims of the Association (hereafter known as ‘the Aims’) are:-

To establish and maintain a democratically elected and representative Committee of Management to facilitate and develop the Association’s Objects & Aims

To provide allotment plots for members and to encourage members to keep their allotments in cultivation, neat & tidy and reasonably weed free, using only organic methods.

To promote the good, environmentally sound cultivation of its members' allotments; encouraging organic gardening and highlighting the value of wildlife on their plots and the allotments overall

To assist members by providing water (during agreed times); maintaining access pathways; maintaining infrastructure for the site and undertaking projects to improve the Allotment site

To encourage the enjoyment of allotment gardening and develop gardening skills and knowledge within members to maintain a high standard of vegetable, flower and fruit cultivation

To be transparent in its operation by openly communicating with members by means of notice boards, newsletters, Facebook and any other suitable means

To promote good relationships with the local community, providing plots for specific community groups and maintaining a fair waiting list system for new allotment holders ensuring, where possible, there is plot availability for people of all backgrounds and abilities

To support the environmentally sound cultivation, development and maintenance of the woodland area

Powers
In furtherance of the Objects & Aims, the Association may:-

Employ and pay staff (who may not be members of the Executive Committee)
Co-operate with other organisations (charities, voluntary bodies, associations and statutory authorities) and exchange information and advice
Take such steps that have been agreed with the Local Authority which are necessary for the good management and cultivation of the allotments and/or woodland
Raise funds by any lawful means except permanent trading
Accept gifts for the general purposes of the Association or for a specific purpose within or connected with the Objects & Aims
Do anything within the law that is necessary in carrying out the Objects & Aims

Membership and Subscription

Membership

Membership is open to any person who wishes to further the Objects & Aims
Every member has one vote at General meetings of the organisation
The Committee may make provision for non-voting categories of membership, including junior membership and set subscription payable (if any)
The Committee shall have the right, for good and sufficient reason, to terminate the membership of any member in writing giving one month or immediate notice, depending on the severity
A member whose membership is so terminated shall have the right to appeal to the Complaints Panel, which will consist of the senior elected members of the Committee (Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, Secretary and Treasurer). Where the Complaints Panel is satisfied after hearing the case put by or on behalf of the member concerned that the member should leave the Association; it may terminate that membership by written notice (written notice will be considered as a letter or email whenever referenced) and that notice is final
A member may resign their membership at any time by contacting the Committee in writing
The Committee must keep a full list of members

Subscriptions

Every member shall pay an annual subscription of such amount as the Committee decides from time to time (at a properly convened Committee meeting) based on the type/size of the plot the member holds
A price will be set for the largest sized plot. A cash amount will then be worked out for smaller plots by working out the percentage size in relation to the largest Plot and then applying the percentage to the fee for the biggest Plot then rounded up to the nearest pound – (e.g. Largest plot A is 150m2 for rental of £50, plot B is 120m2, which is 80% of the size of plot A, so the fee for plot B is 80% of £50 – a fee of £40 would apply to plot B)
The annual subscription is due 1st January each year. Any member who is in arrears at the 31st January without good cause shall be held to have ceased to be a member unless a satisfactory explanation is given to the Committee in writing before this date
Following payment of subscriptions new members will be issued with the gate code for the main gate and woodland gate
Members are not permitted to pass on padlock codes without express, written permission of the Committee
Subscriptions for new members will be prorated in the first year up until the first January 1st as a member, based on when the member signs their Tenancy Agreement
First day of January to last day of March – 100% of subscription
First day of April to last day of June – 75% of subscription
First day of July to last day of September – 50% of subscription
First day of October to last day of December – 25% of subscription

6.3. Plot Fee Refunds

No refunds will be issued after the tenancy agreement has been signed.

Annual and Extraordinary General Meetings

Annual General Meetings (AGM’s)
There must be an AGM of members of the Association every calendar year in January (unless substantial mitigating circumstances prevent this). At the AGM the members will:-

Receive the Committee’s report for the previous year
Receive the Treasurer’s report and accounts for the previous year
Elect the Committee for the following year
Determine any other matter of which notice has been given or which members wish to raise

Extraordinary General Meetings (EGM’s)
EGM’s may be held at any time if called by the Committee, or if at least twenty members of the Association make such a request to the Committee. An EGM must be called within two weeks of such a request being received in writing

Procedures at AGM’s and EGM’s

A General meeting requires fourteen days notice to be given to the members specifying the matters to be dealt with
The Chairperson of the Committee, or in their absence some other person elected by the meeting, will take the chair at General meetings
Every question is decided by a majority of the votes cast, usually by a show of hands. In the case of equality of votes the Chairperson has the casting vote

The Management Committee

The Composition of the Committee

The Committee is the body responsible for the management of the Association
The Committee has the power to make and alter rules for the administration of the Association through normal Committee meetings
The Committee, through normal meetings, has the power to update and/or alter the Tenancy Agreement and Rules of the Association and set plot rates as needed throughout the year
The Committee shall consist of a minimum of three and a maximum of twelve
The members of the Committee are elected annually at the Annual General Meeting and normally hold office until the end of the AGM the following year
The Committee shall have the right, for good and sufficient reason, following a properly proposed and seconded motion that achieves unanimous support, vote members onto the Committee through normal meetings if required to do so
The Committee shall have the right, for good and sufficient reason, to ask any duly elected Committee member to resign following a properly proposed and seconded motion that achieves unanimous support (the affected Committee member is not entitled to vote on this proposal)
A duly elected Committee member who is so requested to resign and a unanimous vote has passed shall have the right to appeal to the Complaints Panel, which will consist of the senior elected members of the Committee (Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, Secretary and Treasurer – should they not be the Committee member the request has been made to). Where the Complaints Panel is satisfied after hearing the case put by or on behalf of the Committee member concerned that they should resign from the Committee; it may terminate Committee membership by written notice (if the Committee member does not want to resign) and that notice is final. The individual will still be a member of the Association and rules, procedures and processes will apply
A member of the Committee who resigns by written notice to the Committee ceases automatically to be a member of the Committee

Officers of the Society

The Officers of the Society will be the Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, Secretary and Treasurer
These Officers will be elected by majority vote by the new Committee members meeting immediately after the end of the AGM; if not elected as part of the AGM or an EGM

Committee Procedures

The Committee must meet at least twice in every calendar year. A special meeting of the Committee may be called at any time on seven days’ notice. A quorum at Committee meetings is three
Every question is decided by a simple majority of the Committee members present and voting at a meeting, usually by a show of hands. In the case of equality of votes the Chairperson of the meeting has a second or casting vote
The Committee must keep minutes of its meetings and proceedings and keep safe all records relating to the Association
The Committee may make rules to govern its own proceedings so long as they are not inconsistent with the provisions of the Constitution
It will be the Secretaries responsibility to ensure a representative of Ashby Woulds Town Council is invited to every Committee and General meeting

Committee Nominations

Members of the Committee are eligible for re-election and those wishing election should be confirmed before the AGM
New candidates who wish to stand for election to the Committee shall make themselves known to the Chairperson before the beginning of business at the AGM. Each nomination should be proposed and seconded
Nominations may also be accepted at the relevant point in the meeting provided the person named is willing to accept the nomination and is proposed and seconded

Finance

All funds belonging to the Association may only be used in furthering the Objects & Aims
The Committee shall maintain an account for the Association at a Bank or Building Society and make regulations governing the signatories (of which there must be at least two) on such accounts. All monies received by the Association shall be held in this account
No member of the Committee shall receive any payment or other benefit from its funds except for reasonable out of pocket expenses properly incurred for the purposes of the Association. ln addition, the Committee reserve the right to award modest honoraria to individual Committee (and other) members in recognition of the work undertaken throughout the year on behalf of the Association. The levels of honoraria to be paid will be determined by the Committee each year
The Committee is responsible for the keeping of books of account and for the preparation of an annual report and annual statement of accounts for the Association

Amendment of the Constitution

The provisions of this Constitution may be amended at a General meeting by resolution passed by two-thirds of the members present but:
Notice of the terms of the proposed amendment/s must be given with the notice calling for the meeting
The Constitution is not an independent document and should be read and considered in conjunction with the ‘Rules of the Association’ and ‘Tenancy Agreement’

Dissolution

The Association may be dissolved at a General meeting by resolution passed by the simple majority of members present
In the event of dissolution, the members of the Committee holding office will remain responsible for the orderly winding up of the affairs of the Association
After paying or making provision for all the debts and liabilities of the Association the Committee shall transfer any remaining assets as appropriate
It will be the responsibility of the Secretary to ensure our landowners (The National Forest Company) and our lease holders (Ashby Woulds Town Council) are updated on dissolution progress

Appendix 1
The Allotments are marked up as ‘A’ and the Woodlands are marked up as ‘W’.
