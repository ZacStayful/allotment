1. Agreement to Let

The Association agrees to let the Tenant a portion of land within the Albert Village Allotments area known as the Allotments from the date stated on this agreement on a rolling yearly basis with the full year starting on January 1st each year. If this agreement is dated partway through the year the first January 1st following signing will begin the rolling nature of the agreement. Rental payments for this let are noted in section 2 of this agreement. The Tenant will have access to the Woodlands as a member of the Association but has no rights to use of this area for any purpose.

The Tenant has agreed to let Plot ______ within the Allotments (hereafter known as the Plot)

2. Rent

The details regarding the provision of rent are covered in the Constitution of the Association in section ‘6. Membership and Subscription’, subsection ‘6.2 Subscriptions (a-f)’.
Points of note:-

Every member shall pay an annual subscription of such amount as the Committee decides from time to time (at a properly convened Committee meeting) based on the type/size of the plot the member holds
A price will be set for the largest sized plot. A cash amount will then be worked out for smaller plots by working out the percentage size in relation to the largest Plot and then applying the percentage to the fee for the largest Plot, then rounded up to the nearest pound – (e.g. Largest plot A is 150m2 for rental of £50, plot B is 120m2, which is 80% of the size of plot A, so the fee for plot B is 80% of £50 – a fee of £40 would apply to plot B)
The annual subscription is due on 1st  January each year. Any member who is in arrears at the 31st January without good cause shall be held to have ceased to be a member unless a satisfactory explanation is given to the Committee in writing before this date
Subscriptions for new members will be prorated in the first year up until the first January 1st as a member, based on when the member signs their Tenancy Agreement
First day of January to last day of March – 100% of subscription
First day of April to last day of June – 75% of subscription
First day of July to last day of September – 50% of subscription
First day of October to last day of December – 25% of subscription

1. Tenant's Agreements

The Tenant agrees with the Association to observe and perform the conditions and obligations set out below and held within the Association Constitution and Rules of the Association accompanying documents that will be issued to the Tenant upon signing this agreement.

Rent
The Tenant must pay the rent reserved, in advance and without deduction otherwise than allowed by statute, on January 1st in each year. Subject to provisions noted in section ‘2. Rent’ of this document.

Use
The Tenant must use the Allotment plot for the production wholly or mainly of vegetables, flowers or fruit crops for consumption by the Tenant and their family and friends
The Tenant must not use the Plot for commercial means or in connection with any trade or business
The Tenant must not grow trees on the Plot other than dwarf varieties producing edible fruit
The Tenant must not have any bonfires on the Plot unless permitted in writing as per section 13 of the Rules of the Association. Whether permitted or not, any bonfire must be properly attended at all times, follow those stipulations in section 13 of the Rules of the Association and must not cause a nuisance or be in breach of any law
The Tenant shall comply with the Rules of the Association. Where there is a conflict between this agreement and the Rules of the Association, then the Rules of the Association will take precedent and be deemed to be in force

Cultivation

The Tenant must keep the whole of the Plot clean, tidy and reasonably weed free. Well manured and otherwise maintain it in a good state of cultivation, fertility and in good condition
The Tenant must keep the soil free from noxious contaminants, livestock carcasses and weeds
The Tenant must keep any pathway included in or abutting on the Plot reasonably free from weeds where not expressly maintained by the Committee
The Tenant must not grow plants that are invasive to the extent of affecting adjoining paths and/or allotment plots

Care of Equipment

The Tenant must take all reasonable steps to protect any water pipes standpipes and water tanks erected within the Allotments or Woodlands
The Tenant must take appropriate steps to avoid wastage of water within the Allotments
The Tenant must take every care to avoid damaging any structures or boundaries erected by other members or the Association and report any damage caused immediately
The Association is not responsible or liable for any loss of or damage to equipment from the Plot, Allotments or Woodland

Nuisance

The Tenant must not cause or permit any nuisance or annoyance to the occupier of any other allotment plot, or the occupiers of neighbouring properties or obstruct or encroach on any path or roadway set out by the Association for the use of any member of the Association with a Tenancy Agreement.

Legal obligations

The Tenant must at all times during the tenancy observe and comply fully with all enactments, statutory instruments, local, parochial or other byelaws, orders or regulations affecting the Allotment Site.

Alienation

The Tenant must not sublet, assign or part with possession of the Plot or any part of it without the express written permission of the Committee
The Association recognises the Plot may be let to an individual as part of/a representative of a community group and will have agreed this at the point of signing and this must be expressly noted on this agreement

Waste

The Tenant must not cut or prune any timber or other trees whether within the Plot or in land adjoining the Plot, or take, sell or carry away any mineral, gravel, sand, earth or clay without the written permission of the Committee
The Tenant must prune any trees growing within the Plot which in the view of the Committee excessively share an adjoining plot
The Tenant must not store materials on the Plot outside of any secure structure that in the view of the Committee presents any form of safety hazard or is excessively untidy

Boundary Structures

The Tenant must keep every hedge that forms part of the Plot properly cut and trimmed and all ditches properly cleaned. Maintain and keep in repair any fences and any gates on the Plot, and use their best endeavours to protect any other hedges, fences or gates in the Allotments of which the Plot forms part or in adjoining land and any notice-board which has been or may at any time during the tenancy be erected by the Association on the Plot, Allotments or Woodlands.

All Plots adjacent to the main fence around the Allotments will have a one metre break between the fence and the exterior of the Plot
The one metre between the fence and Plot must be kept clear by the Tenant
Each Plot will have clear boundaries shown on any side adjacent to another Plot or Allotment fence

Buildings and Structures

The Tenant must not erect any building or structure on the Plot without the express written permission of the Committee
The Tenant must not use bricks and mortar under any circumstances on the Allotments or in the Woodland
The Tenant may not have any building in excess of the dimensions detailed in section 18 of the Rules of the Association. Multiple structures will only be allowed at the discretion of the committee and subject to plot size
The Tenant may not use glass in any construction
Any and all buildings or structures erected on the Plot following permission being given must be kept in good and safe repair and not cause a danger to any other member or visitor.

Refuse

The Tenant must not deposit, or allow other persons to deposit, on the Allotments any refuse or any decaying matter, except manure and compost in such quantities as may be reasonably required for use in cultivation, or place any matter in the hedges, ditches or dykes in the Allotments or Woodland of which the Plot forms part or in adjoining land.

Livestock

The Tenant must not keep any animals or livestock of any kind on the Allotments.

Sprays
Plot holders must only use organic products. Non-organic weed killers or pesticides are strictly prohibited. The tenant must seek out products which are certified organic or soil association approved

Admittance
The Tenant agrees that the Association shall have the right to refuse admittance to the Plot, Allotments or Woodland to any person, other than the Tenant or a member of their family, unless accompanied by the Tenant or a member of their family.

Disputes
The Tenant agrees that any case of dispute between themselves and any other Association member that this will be submitted in writing as covered in section 2 of the Rules of the Association. The Committee’s decision will be final.

Change of Contact Information
It will be the Tenants responsibility to update the Secretary of the Association, in writing, of any changes to their name, address, email or phone number as detailed in this document on page 1, within 28 days of any change occurring.

Yielding up
The Tenant must yield up the Plot at the determination of the tenancy created by this agreement, in section 4 of this document, in such condition as shall be in compliance with the agreements contained in this agreement and in particular remove such buildings, structures, trees or plants from the Plot which in the opinion of the Association is necessary to return the Plot to a re-lettable condition.

Inspections
The Tenant agrees that any duly authorised officer or agent of the Committee may enter and inspect the Plot at any time.

Inspections can happen at any time, however, regular times are planned and expected to take place every four weeks starting in calendar week four each year.
Following Inspection, if the Plot is not deemed to be adhering to the Constitutional requirements, the Rules of the Association or the contents of the Tenancy Agreement then the plot holder will receive a warning in writing from the Committee
At the next inspection if the Plot standard remains the same or there is no significant improvement from the Tenant then the agreement may be terminated as covered in the Constitution of the Association in section ‘6. Membership and Subscription’, subsection ‘6.1 Membership (a-g)’

Special Conditions
The Tenant must observe and perform any special condition the Association considers necessary to preserve the Plot from deterioration of which notice is given to the Tenant in accordance with section 4 of this document.

Lease terms
The Tenant must observe and perform all conditions and covenants that apply to the Plot contained in any lease under which the Association hold the land.

Determination of the Tenancy

Determination on death
This tenancy shall determine at the end of the next full calendar month after the death of the Tenant.

Determination by notice

This tenancy may be determined by the Association giving to the Tenant one months' previous notice in writing
This tenancy may be determined by the Tenant giving to the Association fourteen days previous notice in writing expiring on any day

Determination Where the Allotment has been Appropriated
This tenancy may be determined by re-entry by the Association at any time after giving one months' previous notice in writing to the Tenant on account of the Plot being required:-

For any purpose, other than use for agriculture, for which it has been acquired by the Council for which it has been appropriated under any statutory provision
For building, mining or any other industrial purpose or for roads or sewers necessary in connection with any of those purposes

Determination by re-entry on default
This tenancy may be determined by re-entry by the Association at any time after giving one month's previous notice in writing to the Tenant:-

If the rent or any part of it is in arrears past the 31st January whether legally demanded or not;
If it appears to the Association that there has been a breach of the conditions and agreements on the part of the Tenant contained in this agreement, the Rules of the Association or Constitution and provided that, if such breach is of the conditions or rules affecting the cultivation of the Plot, at least 3 months have elapsed since the commencement of the tenancy
If the Tenant becomes bankrupt or compounds with his creditors
If the Tenant is convicted of theft, criminal damage or any other crime within the Allotments or Woodlands
The Plot is not cultivated for a period of 3 months by the Tenant

Notices

Any notice required to be given by the Association to the Tenant may be signed on behalf of the Association by any Officer of the Society (the Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, Secretary or Treasurer) and may be served on the Tenant either personally or by leaving it at their last known place of abode or by registered letter or letter sent by the recorded delivery service addressed to them there or by fixing the same in some conspicuous manner on the Plot and the Allotment notice board (where there is one). This could also be sent to the Tenant in writing, specifically using email
Any notice required to be given by the Tenant to the Association shall be sufficiently given if signed by the Tenant and showing clearly the Plot Number and sent in a prepaid post letter to the Association Secretary. This could also be sent to the Committee in writing, specifically using email
